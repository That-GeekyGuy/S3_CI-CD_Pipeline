About us

Three people, one roaster, a lot of flour.

The Copper Kettle started as a weekend stall at the Mill Lane market. Enough people kept coming back that in 2019 we took the lease on the old stationery shop next door and never left.

What we care about

  • Beans bought direct from two estates in Chikmagalur.
  • Bread made the night before, never the week before.
  • Ceramic cups. Paper cups only if you ask twice.

The people

  • Meera — roasting and anything involving a ledger.
  • Asif — the ovens, from 4 am.
  • Tenzin — the bar, and the playlist nobody argues with.
